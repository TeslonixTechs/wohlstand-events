What types of events do you handle?+

Wohlstand Events serves weddings, corporate events and social celebrations. Share what you are planning to discuss suitable support.

How early should I book?+

Timing depends on your event date and the support you need. Reach out as soon as you have a date in mind so availability and next steps can be discussed.

Do you travel outside Abeokuta?+

Share your event location in the enquiry form and the team can confirm whether support is available for your destination.

Do you provide event decoration?+

Yes. Event decoration and styling are among the services offered by Wohlstand Events.

Do you handle event-day coordination?+

Yes. Event coordination is available to help the day run smoothly and keep the details moving.

How do I request a quotation?+

Send an enquiry with your event type, date, location and a few details about what you have in mind. The team can follow up to discuss your requirements.

Can I enquire about more than one service?+

Yes. Include the different kinds of support you are considering in your message so the team can understand the full scope of your plans.

What details should I include in my first enquiry?+

Your event type, date, location and approximate guest count are helpful starting points. You can also share your ideas, priorities and questions.
